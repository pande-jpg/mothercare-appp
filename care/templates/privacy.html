MotherCare Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

MotherCare is designed to provide pregnancy-support services such as household assistance, food support, laundry, grocery delivery and qualified healthcare visits.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information such as name, email address, phone number, service address, bookings and account information that you voluntarily provide.

2. Emergency Location

MotherCare may request your device location when you use an emergency feature. Location should be collected only after appropriate user permission and may be used to help coordinate emergency assistance.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used to create and manage accounts, provide requested services, process bookings, communicate with users and coordinate authorized emergency assistance.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be shared with authorized service providers, healthcare professionals, hospitals or emergency partners only when necessary to provide a requested service or emergency assistance and subject to applicable law.

5. Security

MotherCare will use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information. Access should be limited to authorized people who need the information to provide the service.

6. Data Retention

Information will be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for providing services, legal requirements, safety, dispute resolution and legitimate business purposes.

7. User Choices

Users may request information about their personal data and may request correction or deletion where applicable under relevant law.

8. Children's Privacy

MotherCare is intended for adults. We do not knowingly design the service for children.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated when MotherCare's services, technology or legal requirements change.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the MotherCare support team using the contact details provided in the application.
